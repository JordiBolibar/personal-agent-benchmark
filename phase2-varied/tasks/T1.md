### T1 [math]
Consider 1D shallow ice flow on a flat bed on [0, L], so that S = H:

∂H/∂t = ∂/∂x( Γ H^(n+2) |∂H/∂x|^(n-1) ∂H/∂x ) + b(x), with Γ = 2A(ρg)^n/(n+2).

Conditions are H(0,t) = H(L,t) = 0 and H(x,0) = H0(x). A is a constant scalar. The loss is J(A) = ½ ∫ (H(x,T) − Hobs(x))² dx.

Use a Lagrangian to derive the continuous adjoint method:
1. Linearize the flux with respect to H and H_x, keeping every term.
2. Give the adjoint PDE for λ(x,t), with its terminal and boundary conditions. State the direction in which it is integrated.
3. Give dJ/dA as an integral over space and time.
4. Now suppose someone "freezes" the diffusivity, so they treat D as independent of H in the adjoint. Write the adjoint PDE they get and say exactly which terms are missing. Is the result an exact gradient of a different problem, or only an approximation?
5. Say where the derivation needs care: ice margins with H → 0, and points with H_x = 0.

Show all the main steps. End with a short section "Final equations" that lists the results of items 2 and 3.
