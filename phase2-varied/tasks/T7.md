### T7 [ODINN-ecosystem]
You use ODINN.jl to learn how a flow law varies across 10 alpine glaciers over 15 years. Data: yearly gridded thickness with gaps, and velocity maps for 3 of the years. Mass balance comes from either a calibrated `TImodel1` (Muninn, via `calibrate_MB_model`) or a pre-trained `CustomMLP <: MBmodel` from MassBalanceMachine.jl, kept frozen. The forward model is `SIA2D!` from Huginn.

Make a design and justify it:
1. Target: choose between `SIA2D_A_target`, `SIA2D_D_pure` and `SIA2D_D_hybrid`. Give the inputs for the Lux network, and explain how to keep the output physical. Explain what each target can and cannot identify, and which physics each one gives up.
2. Gradient: choose among `EnzymeVJP`, `DiscreteVJP` (Mooncake), `ContinuousVJP` and the `sensealg`/`optim_autoAD` settings in `UDEparameters`. Compare the cost, the accuracy, and the risk when your law or loss changes. Say how you would validate the gradient before a long training run.
3. Loss: design it with `LossH`, `LossV`, `LossHV` or `MultiLoss`. Explain how to handle different units and data gaps, how velocity sampling at only some times interacts with the adjoint, and what can go wrong near ice margins.
4. Mass balance: choose between the two models, and explain how mass balance errors can be wrongly absorbed by the learned law, and how to detect this.
5. List the top three AD pitfalls here, such as mutating caches and `@ignore_derivatives` misuse.

If you are unsure of an exact keyword or API detail, say so instead of inventing it. Use numbered sections, in under 700 words.
